■ d / q axes
The d and q axes are defined by the direction of Ef (the q-axis), not by Vt. The angle that sets the components is the one between Ia and the q-axis, ψ = φ + δ.
COMPARISON
Inductive: ψ is large, so Id is large and positive. It demagnetises, and Ef is the largest of the three.
Resistive: ψ = δ, so Id is small but not zero.
Capacitive: ψ is negative, so Id is negative. It magnetises, and Ef is the smallest.
