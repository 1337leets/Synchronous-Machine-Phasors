PHASOR DIAGRAM
Salient-pole generator, two-reaction theory. Move the controls on the right.
E_f = V + R_a·I_a + jX_d·I_d + jX_q·I_q
E_f = E_q' + (X_d - X_q)·I_d,   E_q' = V + I_a(R_a + jX_q)
Reading the curves. An under-excited machine (left arm) looks inductive and draws reactive power from the grid. An over-excited machine (right arm) looks capacitive and supplies reactive power, like a synchronous condenser. The bottom of each curve is unity power factor and minimum armature current.
